# 🎁 Emotional Gift Intelligence

> Helping people choose emotionally meaningful gifts with confidence—not by searching products, but by understanding relationships, emotions, and the story behind the gift.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Choosing a gift is rarely about finding a product—it's about expressing emotion.

Existing gifting platforms primarily optimize for products, price, and occasions. Users are left scrolling through endless catalogues, uncertain whether a gift truly reflects their relationship with the recipient.

Emotional Gift Intelligence is an AI-powered gifting companion that guides users through a thoughtful conversation to understand **who they're gifting, why they're gifting, and what emotion they want to convey** before generating personalized gift recommendations.

This project explores how conversational AI can make gifting feel intentional rather than transactional.

---

## 🎯 Problem

People often struggle to choose gifts because they ask:

- *What should I buy?*

Instead, they should be asking:

- *What do I want this person to feel?*

Current gifting experiences suffer from several problems:

- Endless browsing creates decision fatigue.
- Recommendations are generic and product-first.
- Relationships and emotions are largely ignored.
- Users lack confidence that they've chosen the "right" gift.

---

## 💡 Solution

An AI-powered gifting guide that understands the context behind a gift before recommending one.

Instead of filtering products, the platform asks meaningful questions about:

- The recipient
- Your relationship
- The occasion
- The emotion you want to communicate
- Personal preferences

It then recommends gifts that feel thoughtful, personal, and emotionally relevant.

## 🛠️ Product Thinking

### Why emotion-first?

Most gifting platforms optimize for:

> Occasion → Category → Product

This product reverses that journey:

> Emotion → Relationship → Context → Gift

The goal is to help users express how they feel—not just purchase an item.
